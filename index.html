I´m a begginer software creator




A year ago, I graduated as a computer technician. For my final project, I had to create a software project of my choice, so I decided to make my first video game. However, one of the requirements was to create a database, so I had to work on two projects at the same time. It was difficult, but I was able to do it.


Noches de horror





A game inspired by the famous Five Nights at Freddy’s, but completely different, while keeping the same idea of surviving each night as time passes. The story is about a young man who suffers attacks from entities during the night. He only has a flashlight and his bedroom to survive.







How did I start?

First, I focused on creating the basic algorithm of the game on paper. I thought about how I could recreate or simulate the movement of the monsters. Then, I implemented the OOP methodology. I created an object that, after a certain amount of time, calls a method that uses a random variable and probability to determine whether the object moves to the next station or not.
